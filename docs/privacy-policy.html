Privacy Policy

Media Sandesh – Android app (com.mediasandesh.news)
Effective date: 29 September 2026

English

This policy explains how the Media Sandesh Android app ("the app") handles information. The app is published by Media Sandesh, a Hindi news publication based in Rudrapur, Udham Singh Nagar, Uttarakhand, India (RNI No. UTTHIN/2012/50365).

Summary

  • The app has no user accounts or sign-in.
  • The app does not use analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and shows no ads.
  • The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone or call logs.
  • We do not sell or rent any information.

1. Information stored only on your device

To remember your choices, the app saves the following in its private storage on your phone. It is never sent to us:

  • Language choice (Hindi / English), theme and text-size settings
  • The city you choose for local news and weather
  • Articles you bookmark or save, and your reading history within the app
  • Your recent search terms

If Android backup is turned on for your phone, this data may be included in your device's own backup to your Google account. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or by uninstalling the app.

2. News tips you choose to send ("Send us news")

If you use the "Send us news" feature, you may enter a headline, location, details, your name, an optional phone number, and attach up to 5 photos or videos that you pick. The app does not upload this to any server. When you tap send, the app hands the content to WhatsApp or to your email app, where you review it and send it yourself to the Media Sandesh editorial desk (WhatsApp +91 94113 40800 / mediasandesh4@gmail.com).

Once sent, the desk receives it like any other WhatsApp message or email, and uses it only to verify and report the news. Your identity can be kept confidential on request. Media files you pick are copied temporarily into the app's private cache so they can be attached, and are not shared with anyone else. Delivery through WhatsApp or email is governed by those services' own privacy policies.

3. Content loaded from the internet

To show news, videos and weather, the app connects to the services below. Like any website, these servers receive standard technical information such as your IP address and device/browser type. The app does not send them any personal information about you.

  • mediasandesh.com: news articles, categories, images and the video gallery
  • YouTube (Google): video lists, thumbnails and embedded video playback (Google Privacy Policy)
  • Google Translate: when you select English, headlines and article text are sent to Google's translation service to be translated
  • Open-Meteo (api.open-meteo.com): weather for the city you selected. Only that city's coordinates are sent, not your location (Open-Meteo terms)

4. Listen (text-to-speech)

The "Listen" feature uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your phone. Article text is processed by that engine according to its provider's policy.

5. Sharing and links

When you share an article or open a link (Facebook, X, WhatsApp, Telegram, YouTube, Instagram, maps, phone or email), the app opens the relevant app or website. What happens there is covered by that service's own privacy policy.

6. Home-screen widget

The optional headlines widget loads the latest headlines from mediasandesh.com. It collects no personal information.

7. Children

The app is a general news app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security

All network connections made by the app use HTTPS encryption.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

1. केवल आपके फ़ोन पर सहेजी जाने वाली जानकारी

आपकी पसंद याद रखने के लिए ऐप ये चीज़ें आपके फ़ोन की निजी स्टोरेज में रखता है। ये हमें कभी नहीं भेजी जातीं:

  • भाषा (हिन्दी / अंग्रेज़ी), थीम और अक्षर-आकार की सेटिंग
  • स्थानीय समाचार और मौसम के लिए आपका चुना हुआ शहर
  • सहेजे गए लेख और ऐप में पढ़ने का इतिहास
  • हाल की खोजें

यदि आपके फ़ोन में Android बैकअप चालू है, तो यह डेटा आपके Google खाते में फ़ोन के बैकअप में शामिल हो सकता है। आप Android सेटिंग में ऐप का स्टोरेज साफ़ करके या ऐप अनइंस्टॉल करके यह डेटा कभी भी हटा सकते हैं।
